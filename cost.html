One reason for Bluetooth's widespread adoption is its relatively low implementation cost. The technology is often empowering devices within a smaller budget, typically between twenty and eight hundred dollars. The Bluetooth feature itself generally adds only a small fraction of the total product cost. Costs vary depending on certification requirements, Bluetooth version, power consumption, audio capabilities, and production volume. Also, Bluetooth Low Energy has further reduced costs by enabling inexpensive battery-powered devices suitable for large-scale IoT deployment.
