Bluetooth is one of the *most* widely used wireless communication technologies in the world. It allows devices to exchange data over short distances without cables and has become a standard feature in smartphones, computers, vehicles, audio devices, and smart-home products. It is limited to short-range communication compared to Wi-Fi and cellular technologies and uses a rather crowded bandwidth which can introduce interference.

Bluetooth has evolved through multiple versions, each improving speed, range, and power efficiency. The technology is managed by the Bluetooth Special Interest Group (SIG), which oversees development, licensing, and promotion.
